Manage Your Account & Data

App: PSC Helper · Developer: ForUniverse

PSC Helper gives you full control over your data. You can either clear your quiz progress (keeping your account active) or permanently delete your account (removing all data). This page explains both options.

Option 1 — Clear Your Quiz Progress

Use this if you want to start fresh while keeping your account active. Your quiz statistics, leaderboard ranking, and accuracy history will be reset to zero — but you remain signed in and can continue using the app immediately.

  1. Open the PSC Helper app on your device
  2. Sign in if you are not already signed in
  3. On the home screen, tap the profile section (your name and email at the top)
  4. Scroll to the Clear Progress section
  5. Tap Clear Progress and confirm your choice

What gets cleared

  • Total questions attempted
  • Total correct answers
  • Your accuracy percentage
  • Your leaderboard position

What stays

  • Your name, email, and profile picture
  • Your account credentials
  • Your sign-in session
⚠️ Clearing progress cannot be undone.

Your statistics cannot be recovered once cleared. You'll start from zero on your next quiz session.

Option 2 — Delete Your Account

Use this if you want to permanently remove your account and all associated data from our servers.

  1. Open the PSC Helper app on your device
  2. Sign in if you are not already signed in
  3. On the home screen, tap the profile section (your name and email at the top)
  4. Scroll to the Delete Account section
  5. Tap Delete Account and confirm your choice

Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted immediately. You will be signed out and returned to the welcome screen.

What gets deleted

  • Your name and email address
  • Your profile picture URL
  • Your quiz progress and statistics
  • Your leaderboard ranking and history
  • Any question reports you have submitted
⚠️ This action cannot be undone.

Once your account is deleted, your data cannot be recovered. You will need to sign in again to create a new account if you wish to use PSC Helper again.

Option 3 — Request Deletion by Email

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us:

Email: foruniverse.dev@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request — PSC Helper

Please include the following details in your email:

  • The email address linked to your PSC Helper account
  • The Google account you used to sign in
  • A brief reason for deletion (optional)

We will process your request and delete your account within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.

What Data Is Retained After Deletion

For legal and security reasons, we may retain certain limited information after account deletion:

  • Anonymous quiz statistics — used for improving the app, with no link to your identity
  • Server logs — retained for up to 30 days for security and abuse prevention

No personally identifiable information is retained beyond the deletion process.

Need Help?

If you have any questions about managing your account or our data practices, please contact us at foruniverse.dev@gmail.com. We typically respond within 48 hours.
